Udyog Manager

A clearer view of everyday business.

Billing, customers and business records together.

Product concept · October 2026

udyogmanager.com

The merchant problem

Daily work creates scattered records.

Bills in one place. Customer dues in another. Expenses recorded late.

Our hypothesis: merchants value a simple way to keep these records together.

Validate through merchant interviews and a working pilot.

The product

One business record across three channels.

Web for a full view. Mobile for daily use. A chatbot for finding information and preparing bills.

All channels use the same business rules and saved records.

Planned capabilities; the product is in development.

The daily workflow

Create a bill. Record a payment. Know what is due.

Add products or services → Review totals → Finalize → Share → Track payment.

Support partial payments and customer statements.

Reprinting the same bill does not create another bill charge.

Who we serve

A shared foundation for many merchants.

Retail shops, wholesalers and service businesses use common billing and record-keeping workflows.

Restaurant, pharmacy and other specialist modules follow in stages.

Broad merchant coverage is the vision; launch scope must be explicit.

The business model

₹1 per finalized bill.

Merchants recharge credits. One successfully issued bill consumes one credit. Customer creation is free.

Drafts, repeat downloads and payment updates do not consume bill credits.

Planned model. Tax treatment, recharge packages and refund terms need approval.

Business visibility

Sales, collections and profit are different.

Show what was sold, what was collected and what customers still owe.

Profit reporting also needs purchase costs, expenses and inventory records where applicable.

Incomplete records should produce a clearly provisional report.

Accountant handoff

Organized records for review.

Plan sales and purchase registers, expense records, GST summaries and supporting exports.

A CA reviews calculations and report mappings before release.

Report preparation is the initial scope; direct filing is a separate integration.

Technology direction

A shared Django business backend.

Next.js web · React Native mobile proposed · FastAPI chatbot · PostgreSQL database proposed.

Django owns invoice totals, permissions, accounting and credit deductions.

The chatbot prepares structured actions; merchants confirm financial changes.

Trust by design

Every bill and credit must reconcile.

Finalize the bill and debit its credit in one database transaction. Retries return the same result.

Separate merchant data, audit financial changes and test backup restoration.

These are implementation requirements, not claims of completed certification.

Launch approach

Prove the daily workflow, then expand.

Discover merchant needs → Build core billing → Pilot with merchants → Improve → Add category modules.

Measure first-bill activation, repeat usage, recharge conversion and support effort.

No launch date or traction is claimed.

Illustrative economics

Usage drives the commercial model.

100 merchants × 300 bills per month × ₹1 = ₹30,000 monthly usage value.

Subtract payment fees, infrastructure, support and AI costs; account for tax and promotional credits.

Arithmetic scenario only. Recharge cash flow and recognized revenue can differ.

Next milestone

A merchant-tested first release.

Confirm pricing terms, recruit pilot merchants and an accounting adviser, then validate the complete billing journey.
